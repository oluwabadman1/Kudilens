Is KudiLens AI available to use yet? +

Not yet. KudiLens AI is an early-stage startup concept. This website includes an interactive front-end demonstration, not a live financial account or service.

Do I need to understand accounting? +

No. The proposed product is designed to explain business finances in everyday language while keeping important calculations transparent.

Will I need to connect my bank account? +

No bank connection is planned for the first version. You'll be able to begin with manually entered business records.

Does KudiLens AI replace an accountant? +

No. Its proposed role is everyday recordkeeping and financial clarity. Professional accounting and tax advice may still be necessary.

What does the demo calculate? +

It adds recorded sales, paid amounts, outstanding balances, item costs and operating expenses. Estimated profit equals sales less recorded product costs and expenses. Figures are fictional, browser-only and reset when the page reloads.
